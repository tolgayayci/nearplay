# Using External Wallets

Connect HOT or Meteor wallet to deploy contracts on NEAR mainnet.

## Supported Wallets

| Wallet | Type | Get it |
|--------|------|--------|
| HOT Wallet | Mobile/Web | [hot.tg](https://hot.tg) |
| Meteor Wallet | Mobile/Web | [meteorwallet.app](https://meteorwallet.app) |

## Connecting Your Wallet

1. Click the wallet button in the toolbar
2. Select **Use External Wallet** from the dropdown
3. Click **Connect Wallet**
4. Choose HOT or Meteor
5. Approve the connection in your wallet

<img src="/images/connect-wallet.png" alt="Connect wallet" />

Once connected, you'll see:
- Your account address
- Your NEAR balance
- Green mainnet indicator

<img src="/images/wallet-connected.png" alt="Wallet connected" />

## Deploying with Your Wallet

1. Compile your contract
2. Click **Deploy**
3. Review the transaction details
4. Approve in your wallet

The deployment costs real NEAR tokens (typically 1-3 NEAR depending on contract size).

<Warning>
Mainnet deployments are permanent and cost real money. Test on Playground mode first!
</Warning>

## Testnet Development

External wallets are **mainnet only**. For testnet:

<Card title="Use Playground Mode" icon="flask" href="/features/deployment">
  Free, instant testnet deployment. No wallet needed.
</Card>

This is by design - HOT and Meteor wallets don't support testnet accounts.

## Switching Back to Playground

1. Open the wallet dropdown
2. Click **Switch to Playground**

You'll return to free testnet deployment mode.

## Disconnecting

1. Open the wallet dropdown
2. Click **Disconnect**

Your wallet is unlinked from NEAR Playground.

## Troubleshooting

**"Transaction rejected"**
- Check you have enough NEAR for gas and storage
- Make sure the contract compiled successfully

**"Wallet not connecting"**
- Refresh the page
- Make sure your wallet extension is unlocked
- Try a different browser

**"Balance shows 0"**
- You might be on a new account
- Send some NEAR to your wallet first
