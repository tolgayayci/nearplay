# Deployment

After compiling, deploy your contract to the NEAR blockchain.

## Deployment Modes

NEAR Playground offers two deployment modes:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Playground Mode" icon="flask">
    **Testnet only**

    - Free deployment
    - No wallet needed
    - Instant setup
    - Perfect for development
  </Card>
  <Card title="External Wallet" icon="wallet">
    **Mainnet only**

    - Uses real NEAR tokens
    - HOT or Meteor wallet
    - You control the keys
    - For production deploys
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Playground Mode (Testnet)

The default mode. Click **Deploy** and your contract is live in seconds.

<img src="/images/deploy-playground.png" alt="Playground deployment" />

Your contract gets an address like:
```
abc123-def456-1705432100.nearplay.testnet
```

<Note>
Playground wallets are funded with testnet NEAR. You can deploy as many contracts as you want for free.
</Note>

## External Wallet (Mainnet)

For mainnet deployments, connect your wallet:

1. Click the wallet button in the toolbar
2. Select **Use External Wallet**
3. Connect HOT or Meteor wallet
4. Click **Deploy**
5. Approve the transaction in your wallet

<img src="/images/deploy-wallet.png" alt="Wallet deployment" />

<Warning>
Mainnet deployments use real NEAR tokens. Make sure you understand the costs before deploying.
</Warning>

### Supported Wallets

| Wallet | Networks |
|--------|----------|
| HOT Wallet | Mainnet |
| Meteor Wallet | Mainnet |

### Deployment Cost

Deploying a contract costs:
- **Storage**: ~0.1 NEAR per 10KB of contract size
- **Gas**: ~0.01 NEAR for the transaction

A typical 150KB contract costs about 1.5 NEAR to deploy.

## After Deployment

Once deployed, you can:

- View your contract on [NearBlocks](https://nearblocks.io)
- Call methods through the Contract Interface
- Share the contract address with others
- Verify the contract source code
